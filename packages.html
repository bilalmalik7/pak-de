Package FAQ

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Every package can be split into milestone-based instalments, for example on signing, on application submission and on admission. Your plan is written into your agreement.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first 20-minute counselling session and the online Profile Analyzer are completely free, with no obligation to buy a package.

Do your fees include university or embassy fees?

No. Third-party fees such as uni-assist, application fees, language exams, the visa fee, blocked account and insurance are paid directly by you to the official provider. We never add a margin on them.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Absolutely. Start with Kickstart or Admission Pro and upgrade anytime. You only pay the difference.
