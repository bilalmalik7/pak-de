Ten clear steps that take you from "Is Germany possible for me?" to unpacking in your new room in Germany. Scroll down and watch your plane take off.

🍂

Winter Semester (Wintersemester)

Classes start September/October. Most applications close around 15 July, and many international master's programmes close earlier (December to May). This is the biggest intake with the most programmes.

Start planning: November–January

🌷

Summer Semester (Sommersemester)

Classes start March/April. Applications typically close around 15 January. There are fewer programmes, but also less competition, which makes it a great option if you're ready early.

Start planning: May–August

Which route is yours?

Your Pakistani qualification decides your starting point. Here's the honest overview.

🎓

Master's in Germany

For a 16-year bachelor's (BS 4 years, BE/BSc Engineering, MBBS, or BA/BSc 14 + MA/MSc 2). This is the most popular route for Pakistani students: English-taught, mostly tuition-free, and 1.5–2 years long.

  • IELTS 6.5 typically
  • Matching subject background
  • SOP + LORs
📚

Bachelor's in Germany

After FSc / ICS / ICom: Studienkolleg (a 1-year foundation) or 1 year of a related BS in Pakistan for direct entry. After suitable A-Levels: direct admission is often possible.

  • IBCC attestation
  • German B1 for most Studienkollegs
  • English-taught options exist
🛠️

Ausbildung (paid training)

Vocational training in nursing, IT, hospitality, logistics and more. You earn a monthly stipend while you learn, and graduates are in high demand.

  • German B1–B2 required
  • Intermediate or higher
  • No CGPA cutoff
