FAQ

German universities: quick answers

How many universities are there in Germany?

Germany has around 400 state-recognised higher-education institutions: research universities, technical universities, universities of applied sciences, art and music academies, and private and church-run universities. Our explorer lists 321 of them.

Which German universities are tuition-free for Pakistani students?

Most public universities outside Baden-Württemberg charge no tuition fees, only a semester contribution. Public universities in Baden-Württemberg charge non-EU students €1,500 per semester, TU Munich charges international tuition, and private universities charge fees. Use the "No tuition" filter above.

Which universities are popular with Pakistani students?

Popular choices include TU Chemnitz, University of Duisburg-Essen, Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg, University of Siegen, Rhine-Waal University of Applied Sciences, Deggendorf Institute of Technology, RWTH Aachen and TU Munich. Switch on the "Popular with Pakistanis" filter to see them all.

Are private universities in Germany recognised?

Many are state-recognised and award German degrees, but not all. Some award degrees from partner universities abroad. Before applying, check the institution's state recognition and its anabin status, and make sure your visa plan fits the programme.
