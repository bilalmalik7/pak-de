Is my degree accepted?

Four checks before you apply to Germany

01
🎓

16 years of education

German master's programmes usually need a 16-year degree: a 4-year BS/BE, or a 14-year BA/BSc plus a 2-year MA/MSc.

02
📜

HEC attestation

Get your degree and transcripts attested by HEC. Universities and the German Embassy ask for attested copies.

03
🔎

anabin status

German universities check your institution in the KMK's anabin database. Most HEC-recognised universities are listed "H+". Check yours before you apply.

04
🧮

German grade

Your CGPA is converted with the Modified Bavarian Formula. Our AI analyzer does it instantly.

FAQ

Pakistani degrees in Germany: answers

Is a Pakistani degree accepted in Germany?

Yes. Degrees from recognised Pakistani universities are widely accepted for admission. Universities check your institution's status in anabin (most HEC-recognised universities are listed H+) and whether your degree meets the programme's requirements, usually 16 years of education for a master's.

What does anabin H+ mean?

anabin is the German database for assessing foreign qualifications. "H+" means the institution is recognised in Germany. "H+/-" means it's recognised only for certain programmes, and "H-" means not recognised. Always check your own university and degree type.

Can I apply to Germany with a 14-year BA or BSc?

Usually not for a master's. Most German master's programmes need 16 years of education. Completing a 2-year MA/MSc or a BS top-up, or starting a bachelor's in Germany, are the common routes.

Do I need uni-assist as a Pakistani student?

Many German universities process Pakistani applications through uni-assist or ask for a VPD (preliminary review documentation). Others accept applications directly. Each university states its own process.
